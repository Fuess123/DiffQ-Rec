Observation:
The real text modal teacher was successfully constructed and used for VQ-VAE training, and modal semantic IDs were generated successfully.
However, under the current 1000-step setup with modal_loss_weight=0.01, both modal-only and morph+modal semantic IDs underperform baseline and morph semantic IDs on text-neighbor overlap.
The modal and morph+modal SID distributions collapse in early hierarchy columns and mainly use the suffix-like last column for uniqueness.
This suggests that the current modal distillation setup does not effectively transfer text-neighbor structure into SID Hamming neighborhoods.
